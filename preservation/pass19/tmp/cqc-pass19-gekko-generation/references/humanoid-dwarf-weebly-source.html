Humanoid Dwarf Gekkos

Humanoid Gekko R-02

 After fighting all of the miniature dwarf gekkos wondering about immediately turn around and go as far back as you can to find a humanoid dwarf gekko.

Humanoid Gekko R-03

 After you have fallen down into the subway by cutting an elevator. Get all the way to the end of the subway, but when you make it to the exit door go right instead to find there is humanoid dwarf gekko.

Humanoid Gekko R-04

 Right before the fight with Sundowner look to your left. You will see a humanoid dwarf gekko on another platform. Use a few rocket launchers to destroy it.

Humanoid Gekko R-05

 After fighting a small group which involves a cyborg with a hammer. Continue on with either a barrel or box and avoid the dwarf gekkos. When you reach the stairs and sliders are about to pass by, just stay still. Wait for them to leave then continue all the way down the stairs to find the humanoid dwarf gekko.

Humanoid Gekko R-07

 At the start of the mission drop down and head right immediately. Continue across the road to the other side to find a humanoid dwarf gekko waiting for you.
