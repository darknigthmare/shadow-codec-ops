Konami release new Metal Gear Rising assets

On December 11, 2012 by Ash Meehan

Konami has released new screenshots and videos for next year’s Metal Gear Rising: Revengeance.

The screenshots which can be seen below features the various cyborgs you will be fighting in the game.

[Show slideshow]


Konami has released two new Metal Gear Rising preview trailers. The first trailer features a Metal Gear RAY Boss Battle Gameplay Preview and the second trailer features a Mastiff battle gameplay preview.

Metal Gear RAY Boss Battle Gameplay Preview

Mastiff Battle Gameplay Preview

Metal Gear Rising: Revengeance will be available February 19, 2012 on the PlayStation 3 and Xbox 360 in North America, February 21, 2013 in Europe, and February 22, 2013 in the UK.
