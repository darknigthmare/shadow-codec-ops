Quiet from Metal Gear Solid in the GA-HQ Video Game Character DB

 Quiet is a non-playable character from Metal Gear Solid V: The Phantom Pain (2015) where she can accompany Venom Snake (Big Boss) but is also a boss he has to battle.

She can only breathe through her skin, therefore she can’t be fully clothed because that would slowly suffocate her. Quiet uses only Sniper Riffles in MGSV, if she becomes a buddy of Venom Snake, she can Scout and Attack.
