Original Thread: Contractual Obligation: Let's Play MGS1 + MGS4 [VLP]

 

Introduction



Hello! Get ready for a really long ass back-to-back LP of both MGS1 and MGS4. We'll be playing the original MGS for most of the first LP, but there will be times where we compare it to its Gamecube remake, Twin Snakes.

Dual-Track Commentary
Most of these videos have dual-track commentary. The default track has cut commentary so you can watch the cutscenes in peace. The second track is uncut commentary where we talk over everything. To listen to the second track, the video and play it in either VLC or Media Player Classic. You can navigate through the menus and select Track 2. If you're using Media Player Classic, just press the A key to switch audio tracks.
