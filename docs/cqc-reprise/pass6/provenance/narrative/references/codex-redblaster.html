Background

Red Blaster is a elite assassin, who is a master at throwing grenades. He achieved a special ops degree in Rumumba University in the USSR and later became a Spetsnaz member. He would eventually become a mercenary and would join Big Boss's Zanzibar Land. During his time at Zanzibar Land, he would set up a trap for and later ambush Solid Snake on the 30th floor of the Tower Building. Despite his traps, he would lose the fight with Snake and would be killed.
