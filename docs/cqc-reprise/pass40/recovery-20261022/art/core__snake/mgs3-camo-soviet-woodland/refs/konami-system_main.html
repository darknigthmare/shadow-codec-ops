  · Cure >>
Life's biggest mission to "live through".

 Mother natures surrounds you from all directions in the "jungle". The most important thing while placing yourself under the tough conditions of an infiltration mission is obtaining food to survive. If the "stamina gauge" -- symbol of all life activities -- goes low, your healing power goes down, you cannot aim your gun properly, and your overall mission becomes harder to accomplish.
The jungle setting abounds with mother nature's gifts -- snakes, birds, fish, fruit, etc. Find your prey in the grass, branches, and in water and use your gun, knife, and traps to capture them for food.
You must also keep in mind changes in the climate such as rain and temperature to maintain your stamina.





  · Close Quarters Combat >>
"Close Quarters Combat" -- a fighting technique against nearby enemies.

 In the jungle where your view is obscured, enemies can jump right in front of you suddenly without your noticing them till the very moment. You might not be able to use your artillery because of rocks and trees that come in your way. CQC (Close Quarters Combat) is a fighting technique effective against enemies that are right near you. While holding both a handgun and combat knife, you grab the approaching enemy and throw them. Simple controls allow you to use the enemy as a shield or threaten them for valuable information.
By combining CQC with middle and long-range battle (with weapons obtained through the mission), you can create your own fighting style in those battles that are not always avoidable.





  · Camouflage Index >>
Blend into nature with "disguise" and fool the enemy eye.

 Wear camouflage gear that matches your environment and apply face paint to fool the enemy eye. Becoming one with your surroundings is the most effective way to hide yourself from the enemy.
By using disguise that matches the jungle, swamp area, indoor environments, daytime and nighttime, you can improve your chance of surviving drastically. Simply hiding behind something in extreme circumstances in enemy territory might not be enough. Constantly being aware of your environment, or "knowing nature", is the shortcut to completing your mission successfully. Into what colors will the warriors change during their endeavors to survive the Cold War?
