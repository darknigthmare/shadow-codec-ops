# P41 Gala — independent source-art observations

Observer: `/root/p41_authored`. This is a qualified artistic observation report,
not a Root approval, runtime admission, anatomical certificate, or assertion of
retail animation fidelity. All nine characters' RIGHT main, replacement LEFT,
and selected supplement pages were physically viewed as original images. The
four unarmed sets were re-viewed together with their full-body supplements;
the five equipped sets were re-viewed with their weapon supplements. No raster
pixels were edited, mirrored, restored by crop, or duplicated into new roles.

The Gala clothing and fighting choreography are original CQC adaptations for
each exact incarnation. They must remain labelled as such. Separate eras are
not merged. Existing native generated art is an identity reference, not retail
evidence. Actual publisher references were also viewed for Campbell MG2,
Campbell MGS1, Mei Ling MGS1, Nastasha MGS1, Rose MGS2 and Holly MG2. Actual MGS4
cutscene references for Campbell and Mei Ling were additionally obtained and
viewed after generation. An independently obtained original-retail Otacon MGS4
full-body reference remains unavailable in this handoff; his current native
same-era CQC source was viewed instead. Do not erase that qualification.

- **Nastasha MGS1:** recognisable adult face, short golden-blond side-swept bob,
  black trouser tuxedo and complete dress shoes. Both views, high kick, forward
  reach, low punch, flat head-resting KO and recovery are materially different
  drawings. Replacement right recovery supplies complete legs missing from the
  historical main-page recovery. Several LEFT main poses retain a cigarette,
  despite the generation prompt asking for empty hands. A cigarette is visible
  in the publisher character reference, but its use during these invented CQC
  actions is an artistic adaptation; it is not an attested retail choreography.
  All four source pages have a whole standing neutral with visible crown and

- **Rose MGS2:** adult long brown/auburn hair and civilian identity remain
  consistent with the physically viewed Konami artwork. Gala replaces the
  original civilian clothes rather than changing her era. Both full neutrals,
  crouch, airborne tuck, punch, forward reach, kick, low recon reach, backward
  hurt and flat KO are visually readable. Replacement recoveries and low tuck
  include their legs and hands. All four page neutrals are whole. Recommend
  Root inspection now, with ordinary drawn-density and action-framing checks.
- **Otacon MGS4:** thin rectangular spectacles, medium brown hair, adult slim
  body and black gloves match the viewed existing MGS4 CQC base. The new formal
  jacket, trousers and shoes are coherent across both directions. The right
  reach is open-handed; the left forward reach has more pointed fingers, a
  minor authored pose difference requiring review rather than a retail-throw
  claim. All four page neutrals and replacement recoveries are whole. Recommend
  Root inspection now, subject to the explicit independent-retail-reference

For these three, using each page's own full neutral is a bounded initial
display-density choice. It does not prove identical head/torso proportions in
every independently drawn pose. Root must still inspect the final framed job.

## Animation and scale limits shared by all nine

The basic walk drawings are independently painted, but several main and
supplement steps have similar leg extension. A complete alternating anatomical
gait cycle has **not** been demonstrated. The appropriate present claim is a
basic pair of independently drawn steps, as exposed in each concept's gait
scope, not 1:1 retail animation. Low role15 is a compact tuck/kneeling recovery;
it is not an entire completed somersault. Role14 is an actual flat, head-resting
KO figure in both directions. Its projected length, head size and body framing
still need final Root review; bounding-box width is not a stature measurement.

The proposed X axes were manually chosen visually, with approximately 20 native
pixels of stated review tolerance. They deliberately follow the body rather
than forward hands, guns, canes or averaged feet. They remain proposals until
the exact final job is viewed and approved. Source ownership counters support
pixel preservation; they cannot certify face identity, anatomy or movement.

Runtime hand sockets V2 identify visible alpha>=200 fingertip pixel centres in
the owned body, excluding the already detached grenade component. Muzzle and
rear-barrel points belong to the selected actual gun drawing. These geometric
proposals still require source-point and transformed-emitter review. No socket
or inferred gameplay result is approved by this report.
